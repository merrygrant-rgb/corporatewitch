How To Use This Bank (The Batch Strategy)

You said you'd "batch heavy monthly, light weekly." Here's the workflow that makes this realistic:

  • First Saturday of the month (2 hours): Pick 12 hooks. Film 8 TikToks back-to-back. One outfit, one lighting setup, done.
  • Every Sunday (30 minutes): Take 1 TikTok, adapt it for LinkedIn (witchy language dialed down 40% — that's the "Weekly Workflow" voice). Schedule for Tuesday 8am.
  • Every Wednesday (15 minutes): Repost best-performing TikTok to Instagram Reels. That's it.
  • Rule: LinkedIn versions replace "spell" with "workflow," "coven" with "community," "witch" with "veteran." Same soul, different dialect.

One more thing: Click any hook's "Copy" button to grab the full script — hook + body + CTA already formatted for you. Open a note, paste, tweak. Don't reinvent.
